How the Embedded Navigation Control Works: The Smart Farm Bot follows an agricultural navigation track using optical contrast:
  • Line Detection: The black rubber/paint line absorbs infrared photons, returning low reflection (Digital 1). The lighter surrounding soil reflects IR strongly back to the phototransistor (Digital 0).
  • Centering Rule: When both sensors read 1, the bot is centered on the path and both motors drive at equal PWM.
  • Differential Steering: If the bot drifts right, the right sensor departs the line into soil (Left=1, Right=0). The ESP32 immediately slows the left motor and maintains high PWM on the right motor, creating an instant pivot torque to the left until centered.
  • Safety Recovery: If both sensors lose the line (0, 0), the bot brakes and sweeps left/right searching for the lost line rather than blindly driving into crops.
